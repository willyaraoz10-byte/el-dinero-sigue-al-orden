# El Dinero Sigue al Orden

Open source system to organize your finances with the 7 rooms method.

This project is based on the book El Dinero Sigue al Orden: Las siete habitaciones que transforman tu economia, by Willy Araoz, and on a YouTube channel with more than 400 videos about money, order, abundance and mindset.

## The problem

Most personal finance content in Spanish is either purely motivational or purely technical. There was no practical, open system that connects mindset with daily financial order through a simple framework anyone can apply at home.

This repo provides that system for free, so anyone can use it, improve it and adapt it.

## What is inside

1. The 7 rooms method: a simple way to organize your financial life by areas, like rooms in a house. Each room has a clear order to follow.
2. Templates: checklists and trackers to keep your money in order every week.
3. Guides: step by step guides in Spanish to apply the method at home.
4. Content system: resources to help creators and educators teach financial order in their communities.

## Who is it for

- People who want to organize their money without complicated apps
- Spanish speaking creators, educators and community leaders
- Readers of the book and followers of the YouTube channel
- Anyone who wants a simple, practical system for abundance and order

## How to use it

1. Read the guides in this repo.
2. Download the templates.
3. Start with one room at a time.
4. Repeat every week to keep the order.
